# FireFly: First Light

A short film you scroll through. Everything on the site, every animation, colour and line of copy, serves this one story.

## Logline

A small robot called Nav, the club's first machine, wakes up in the dark. Together with a crew of seventeen students it crosses five chapters of the club and turns a question into something that works. You are the next to come aboard.

## The idea behind it

FireFly is a ship, and the unknown is the sea. The wind is curiosity. The sail is the mark that raises in the loader. The club is where raw questions get turned into structure, colour and, finally, work that ships. The visitor is not a spectator; they are the next crew member, and the last scene is an invitation.

## The film, scene by scene

The site is five acts. Each section below is one scene, with what the viewer sees, what it means, and which part of the site it is.

### Cold open: Ignition (loader)
Black screen. A bar fills while a counter rolls: the ship's systems coming online. At 100 the sail mark builds itself in the middle of the screen, piece by piece. Then the black hull splits open along a diagonal, like a hatch.
The same hatch closes and opens between every page, with the name of the destination above the counter, so travelling to another page always feels like a short jump through the ship.
*Meaning: the club powering up. The sail is raised before anything else happens.*

### Act I: The Swarm (hero)
The hatch opens on a dark window full of drifting shapes: the Ideas, in white, violet, black, teal and one gold. They respond to your cursor: push them and they scatter, collide and settle. The line above: *We build intelligent systems and bring AI ideas to life with the SRM community.*
*Meaning: before the club, there is only a cloud of ideas. Touching them is the first act of curiosity.*

### Act I, continued: The Question (intro and reel)
As you scroll, a cyan ribbon plots a course across the page: the route the ship will take. The heading says *Bold ideas, built to think.* A photo of the crew at work shifts from blue-grey to colour as it comes into view. Then the Reel card slides in tilted, straightens as it reaches the middle of the screen, and scrolls away with the page: the mission recording.
*Meaning: ideas become a question, and the question gets a crew and a plan.*

### Act II: The Cargo (featured work)
Project tiles, each tagged with its domain and status. They bend with your scroll speed.
*Meaning: what the crew has already brought back from earlier voyages.*

### Act III: The Voyage (the voyage)
The longest scene. A console appears (a tablet frame) showing Nav standing on Earth at sunrise. The heading says *Inside FireFly, a mind is waking up.* We scroll and the console grows until we are inside it. Nav lifts off the planet, a ring of light opens, and three lines of words (*Come aboard. Follow the signal. Ask what's next.*) burst outward past the camera. The screen fades to black and the first corridor appears. Then five chapters, one per domain, each a different space with its own colour. Nav is always in frame, small against the scale, and he reacts to each space: he flies ahead on a teal thruster, leaning into the flight and doing a barrel roll or flip at every chapter change, dances in the bright room, drifts calmly through the open worlds, nods yes in the last chapter, then waves and gives a thumbs-up at the end.

| Chapter | Domain | Space | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I. Build | Technical Core | A dark steel cage with data glitching along the beams and a soft gold glow in one corner | Structure comes first: rigor and engineering |
| II. Discover | R&D | A dense woven vortex of teal, violet and gold light strands spiralling inward around a silk wall, with motes streaming through it | A network forming into something that looks like art, not a diagram. Research is flying toward what nobody has seen |
| III. Create | Spatial Design | A blue room of white rings and arches that ends in a pane of glass | Colour and light arrive. Design makes it feel alive |
| IV. Connect | Corporate and Operations | The glass shatters and we float through a field of stickers (a smile, a gear, a star, a chip, a rocket): the people and tools of other crews | Other crews and companies: bright minds we can reach |
| V. Record | Operations and Media | Open space again, Nav alone, waving | The voyage is written down. The robot says goodbye |

As you scroll, each chapter title rises letter by letter from the bottom of the screen, holds, then sinks away as the next one arrives, with the domain name above it and a one-line meaning below. A five-step rail on the right edge tracks the voyage, and a "Next chapter" tag appears near the end of every chapter. At the end Nav waves goodbye in open space. One button, bottom right: *Explore the chapters.*
*Meaning: this is how a question becomes a result, and how a machine gets a mind. The film is called First Light because the robot and the glass mind finally meet.*

### Act IV: The Call (join, footer)
The fluid glow behind the final heading is the wake of the ship. *The crew has a seat for you.* The button says *Join the club!* The footer is the harbour: address, links, credit.
*Meaning: the film ends where you begin.*

## Motion language

Every piece of text arrives in its own way, each time it scrolls into view for the first time: lines rise out of an invisible mask, letters rise one after another, words blur in, long statements brighten word by word as you scroll, small labels scramble like a console printing. Cards rise from below, left from the left and right from the right, at the same moment. Marquees of outlined words keep the page moving, and speed up when you scroll. Nothing shakes while you scroll; everything that moves is driven by one frame loop.

## Rules so everything fits

1. **Always forward.** The camera only moves forward during the voyage.
2. **Chaos to order to light.** Each act is a step from loose ideas, to structure, to colour, to work.
3. **Touch changes the world.** Anything the viewer can move (ideas, trail, cursor) reacts, because curiosity is the theme.
4. **One meaning per colour**, as in the table above.
5. **No text without a job.** Every line is mission statement, caption or call to action.
